In the workshop

Nothing about a Tales Co. bear is mass-produced

Every bear is cut, stuffed, and stitched by one of six makers working in small batches — usually no more than a dozen bears a week between them. Seams are hand-sewn, joints are cotter-pinned so limbs actually move, and no two bears from the same pattern come out quite the same.

  • Mohair & alpaca coats

    Sourced from small European mills, hand-carded before cutting.

  • Hand-blown glass eyes

    Made in small batches by a glassworker in Oaxaca.

  • Cotter-pin joints

    Arms, legs, and head move and hold a pose — no glued limbs.

  • Wool felt paw pads

    Hand-stitched, one uneven line at a time — that's the point.
